Ease of use

Touch targets

At 1024 px and below, or on any touch screen, every control is at least 44 px high: buttons, inputs, menu rows, tabs, tick boxes and the sidebar. On a laptop with a mouse they stay at 36 px. Narrow this window to see the change; the sizes are tokens (--h-control, --h-menu, --h-nav, --h-row).

Undo, not confirm

A reversible action happens at once and a toast offers "Undo" for seven seconds (Ctrl Z works too). Nothing is sent until the toast goes. A confirm dialog is kept for what cannot be taken back, such as cancelling a bill. UndoService.offer({ message, commit, undo }).
